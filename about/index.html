About this site

A public reference for difficult BSV questions.

BSV.place exists to make serious evaluation easier: state the concern fairly, link the strongest available sources, and leave important limits visible.

Who maintains it

BSV.place is maintained by Project Babbage and contributors. Project Babbage builds BSV-related software, which creates relevant experience and a potential bias. Readers should evaluate the linked evidence rather than treating the maintainer as authority.

What it is not

This is not an official publication of the BSV Association, a token promotion, investment advice, or legal advice. No belief about the identity of Satoshi Nakamoto is required to use the site.

How claims are handled

Primary records, current code, reproducible tests, and independently checkable measurements carry more weight than advocacy. Each answer distinguishes demonstrated capability from adoption and commercial success.

How to challenge it

Send the exact sentence you dispute, the best public source available, and the correction you believe follows. Material factual corrections are recorded publicly.
