Is BSV scaling real, or just a datacenter claim?

“Large blocks trade decentralization for a few industrial nodes, and headline throughput is marketing rather than independently reproducible capacity.”
Short answer

The answer without the theater.

BSV deliberately optimizes for high transaction volume, low fees, SPV clients, and specialized block-producing nodes. That is a real architecture, not a magic removal of resource costs. Vendor or test-network throughput should be labeled as such; it does not by itself prove sustained mainnet demand, broad node diversity, or independent reproduction.

What is true

The objection's strongest ground

  • Larger blocks increase bandwidth, storage, propagation, and operational requirements for block-producing and archival infrastructure.
  • The design expects most users and applications to verify relevant transactions with headers and Merkle proofs rather than operate archival block-producing nodes.
  • A high test benchmark, a large historical block, low current fees, and sustained economic throughput are four different facts.
What remains unresolved

Do not claim more than the evidence

  • This site does not treat a published Teranode benchmark as independent validation unless methods and reproduction are inspectable.
  • Cheap transactions do not prove useful demand. Capacity is not adoption.
  • SPV reduces client burden; it does not make block production free or guarantee competitive mining markets.
Inspect it yourself

A better next move than arguing

  1. Measure current mainnet blocks, fees, transaction mix, orphan/reorganization behavior, and miner distribution.
  2. Ask whether a benchmark publishes hardware, topology, workload, error rate, duration, and reproducible tooling.
  3. For an application, compare the cost of the actual transaction and proof workflow—not a headline transactions-per-second number.
Falsifiability

What would change this answer?

Independent sustained tests, broader production participation, and transparent cost curves would strengthen the claim. Unreproducible benchmarks or deteriorating operator concentration would weaken it.
