Plain-language privacy

Awareness without surveillance.

BSV.place is a static evidence desk. Reading does not require an account, a wallet, a cookie banner, or an advertising identity.

Anonymous measurement

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate page visits, referrers, performance, and campaign traffic without advertising identifiers. Project Babbage's Usercom service records bounded editorial events such as source clicks, copied answers, copied links, correction activity, and searches that returned no results.

The search phrase itself is not included in the zero-result event. Search filtering happens entirely in the browser.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers or cross-site profiles.
  • No wallet addresses, balances, transaction details, or private keys.
  • No identity credentials, session replay, or browser fingerprinting.
  • No free-text search contents.
  • No sale or rental of reader information.

Editorial submissions

Correction and objection forms send the text you enter, the selected page or category, an optional public evidence URL, and optional contact details to Project Babbage's editorial intake service.

Contact information is used only to clarify that submission. Do not include secrets, private wallet information, confidential records, or sensitive personal data.

Public accountability

Accepted factual corrections are summarized in the public revision log. A submitter's name or contact information is not published without specific permission.

Questions or deletion requests

Email hello@projectbabbage.com and identify the editorial submission involved. Aggregate analytics and anonymous event records are not designed to identify a reader. This policy was last reviewed July 11, 2026.
