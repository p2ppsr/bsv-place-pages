Plain-language privacy

Awareness without surveillance.

You can read BSV.place without an account, a wallet connection, or an advertising profile.

Search and reusable resources

Search terms and format, topic and depth filters are processed in your browser. Guide downloads and the public library export are ordinary static files; using them does not require a wallet, identity or account. This release adds no cross-site reader tracking or automatic social posting.

Basic site measurement

We use privacy-focused measurement to understand which pages are useful and whether the site is working properly. It does not create an advertising profile or require a login.

Technical details

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate visits, referrers, campaign attribution, and performance information. A Project Babbage service counts a small set of actions such as opening a source, copying an answer, sharing a page, or submitting a correction. Search filtering happens in your browser; the words you search for are not included in those records.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers or cross-site profiles.
  • No wallet addresses, balances, transaction details, or private keys.
  • No identity credentials, session replay, or browser fingerprinting.
  • No free-text search contents.
  • No sale or rental of reader information.

What happens when you contact us

Correction and question forms send the text you enter, the selected page or subject, an optional public evidence URL, and any contact details you choose to provide to Project Babbage.

Contact information is used only to clarify that submission. Do not include secrets, private wallet information, confidential records, or sensitive personal data.

Public accountability

Accepted factual corrections are summarized in the public revision log. A submitter's name or contact information is not published without specific permission.

Questions or deletion requests

Email hello@projectbabbage.com and identify the submission involved. Aggregate site measurements are not designed to identify a reader. This policy was updated October 5, 2026.
